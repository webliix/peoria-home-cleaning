About Peoria Home Cleaning

Why Homeowners Choose Us

Living in Peoria and the surrounding desert valley means dust, sand, and pollen constantly settle across hard surfaces, carpets, ceiling fans, and vents. Our network of experienced cleaning professionals utilizes advanced microfiber tools, hospital-grade non-toxic disinfectants, and multi-stage HEPA filtration vacuums to preserve your home’s air quality and beauty.

Methodical Process

Systematic Cleaning Standards

Every room receives specialized care using color-coded microfiber towels and eco-friendly formulas.

🍳 Kitchen Area Protocol

We sanitize countertops, polish stainless steel appliances, scrub sinks and faucets, degrease backsplashes, and mop tile floors to eliminate food oils and odors.

🚿 Bathroom Hygiene Protocol

We eliminate hard water scale and soap scum from glass shower doors and tubs, sanitize toilet fixtures inside and out, clean mirrors streak-free, and disinfect tile floors.

🛋️ Living & Dining Protocol

We remove dust from coffee tables, shelving, ceiling fan blades, and blinds. Upholstery is neatly organized, and all floors are thoroughly vacuumed and mopped.

🛏️ Bedroom & Sleeping Protocol

We dust all nightstands, lamps, and furniture. Carpets are deep-vacuumed with HEPA filtration to lower allergens, and beds are neatly styled or refreshed upon request.
